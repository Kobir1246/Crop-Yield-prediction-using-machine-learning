How to Use
  1. Select a crop, district, and season
  2. Enter field parameters (area, temperature, humidity, rainfall)
  3. Choose a model (XGBoost or Stacking Ensemble)
  4. Click "Predict Yield" to see results with SHAP explanations
  5. Use "Clear All" to reset the form for a new prediction
